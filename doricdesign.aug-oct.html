04

What worked, what needs fixing

What the three months of numbers point to.

Keep doing

  • ↗

    Expert-led reels with a clear hook. The price-point interior reel reached 193K views. That's a format to repeat, not a one-off.

  • ↗

    Process & transformation content. The "dream home" and "3D to reality" reels got the most shares (45 and 28). People save and forward these.

  • ↗

    Real client voices. The Fasalu testimonial had shares equal to its likes, a sign of high trust.

  • ↗

    Lead & chat campaigns. 11 campaigns ran this quarter. September's campaigns brought results at ₹31–44 each, the cheapest of the quarter.

  • ↗

    Delivering above scope. 22 pieces in August and 19 vs 14 in September kept the feed active.

Fix next

  • !

    Organic reach fell 42% and interactions fell 52% in October. The profile grid is mostly static posters, while reels are doing the work.

  • !

    Net −89 followers. Unfollows (585) beat new follows (496). Big ad reach isn't turning into people who stay.

  • !

    Lead quality is unknown. 597 leads, but no converted, lost or qualified data. Cost per real client can't be measured yet.

  • !

    Facebook is paid-only. 99% of its views came from ads, and organic was just 2,435.

  • !

    Fewer conversations. Messaging contacts dropped 23%, so the profile and stories need stronger "DM / WhatsApp us" prompts.

06

November: what we upgrade

Instagram-first. Build repeatable reach, attract the right audience, and turn interest into qualified design enquiries.

Core objective

Build repeatable reach, attract the right audience and turn interest into qualified design enquiries.

REACHFOLLOWDM / WHATSAPPQUALIFIED LEAD

Content & community

What goes on the feed

  1. Prioritise expert-led reels, project walkthroughs and client proof. Reels already drive 87% of organic views.

  2. Run recurring series: design tips, before-and-after, 3D vs reality and NRI home planning.

  3. Educational carousels on cost, layouts, materials and planning, built to be saved and shared.

  4. Daily Stories for work-in-progress, polls, Q&A and enquiry prompts.

Profile & conversion

Turning visitors into enquiries

  1. Sharper bio CTA with a direct WhatsApp / enquiry link.

  2. Organise Highlights (Projects, Process, Client Reviews, Contact) and pin the strongest proof-led posts.

  3. Show reels on the main grid so the profile looks like a video portfolio, not a wall of posters.NEW

  4. Track profile visits, link taps, DMs, WhatsApp enquiries and qualified leads.

Ads & lead quality

Spend where enquiries come from

  1. Turn winners into lead ads. Run the 193K price-hook reel and the before/after reels as lead-form and WhatsApp creatives.NEW

  2. Retarget warm audiences: October's video viewers and profile engagers, with a "plan your home" lead form.NEW

  3. Keep most of the budget on lead & message campaigns (September's ₹31–44 range). Use reach budget only to push new reels.NEW

  4. Mark every lead in the Leads Center as contacted, qualified, converted or lost, so we report cost per qualified lead.NEW

Retention & Facebook

Turning reach into a following

  1. Review follower growth and top content weekly, not only at month-end.

  2. Every reel ends with a reason to follow: the next part of the series, the next home tour.NEW

  3. Cross-post reels natively to Facebook to build organic views beyond the 2,435 from October.NEW

  4. Reply to comments and DMs the same day. Faster replies bring more conversations back.NEW
