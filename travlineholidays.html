The gap

35,000 happy travellers offline. 611 followers online.

A new customer comparing agencies on Instagram or Google cannot see two decades of experience. That is the gap this plan closes.

Customs check

Digital audit

What we found on travlines.in and @travlineholiday, stamped as cleared or needing work.

Cleared Website

Looks premium and easy to use

  • Clean hero with a trip finder
  • Separate numbers for group, domestic and custom tours
  • WhatsApp button always visible
  • Rich, well written destination copy
Needs work Website SEO

Google has very little to rank

  • Every section sits on one URL, so no page exists for "Georgia tour from Kochi"
  • No meta description, and the title is only the brand name
  • Around ten H1 headings on one page
  • No blog or travel guides yet
Needs work Trust signals

Proof is hidden

  • One testimonial for 35,000 travellers
  • No Google rating or review widget
  • No starting prices, every card says "Enquire"
  • No real traveller photos or trip galleries
Needs work Instagram

Great hooks, too few posts

  • 611 followers and 56 posts in total
  • Only about three posts in October
  • Mostly posters, very few reels with real faces
  • Bio has no clear call to action
Cleared Content ideas

The offbeat angle is gold

  • "Stop visiting only Almaty, try Mangystau" is a winning hook
  • Arctic, Harbin and Spiti stand out from typical Kerala agencies
  • Neat destination highlights already in place

A namesake abroad

  • A Sri Lankan operator also trades as Travline Holidays, with 26 Tripadvisor reviews
  • Searches outside Kerala may mix the two
  • A strong Google Business Profile with reviews fixes this
The big idea

Go where Kerala hasn't been yet.

Position Travline as Kerala's go to company for offbeat and expedition travel, while still owning the classics. Every trip, every traveller and every departure becomes content.

What we will post

Six content pillars

A mix of reels, carousels and stories in Malayalam and English, planned a month ahead.

01

The Offbeat Atlas

Short reels on places most Malayalis have never heard of, with a clear "we can take you" hook.

"Stop visiting only Almaty. Try Mangystau."
02

Departure Day

Real groups at Kochi airport, on the trip and back home. Faces build trust faster than posters.

Group send offs, trip vlogs, welcome back clips
03

Traveller Stories

Video testimonials from past customers. Twenty years of happy travellers, finally on camera.

"Our Kashmir trip was absolutely wonderful"
04

Fixed Departures

Dates, seats left and starting price in one clean post. Gives followers a reason to message today.

"Vietnam · 12 Jan · 8 seats left · from ₹XX,XXX"
05

Travel Truths

Visa tips, packing lists and budget breakdowns in simple Malayalam. Useful content people save and share.

"Georgia visa for Indians, explained in 30 seconds"
06

Presenter Led Reels

Our on camera presenter turns itineraries into stories, giving Travline a recognisable face and voice.

"5 days in Uzbekistan, here's how it goes"
Route map

Where enquiries will come from

Instagram and Facebook

Consistent reels and posts, a sharper bio with WhatsApp link, and highlight covers per destination.

Meta Ads

A campaign for each group departure, with WhatsApp and lead form ads aimed at Kerala and the GCC Malayali audience, plus retargeting.

Google Business Profile

Full profile setup, weekly updates and a review drive with past travellers, so "Travline Holidays Kochi" shows the right company with stars.

Website SEO

Proper meta tags and a page for each key package, so Travline ranks for searches like "Spiti tour from Kerala".
