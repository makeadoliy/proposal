1. 11:47 PM

Every Tuesday, one real question sent to Future Fair late at night, shown with the time it arrived and an honest answer.

  1. Pick the most common worry from the week's DMs
  2. Hide the sender's name, keep their words
  3. Answer like a counsellor, not like an advert
CaptionThe questions that keep you awake are the ones we answer every day. Send yours. No question is too small.

Why it works: It shows the late night worry students feel and proves Future Fair listens. It also trains followers to send DMs.

2. My Second Choice

Former students hold a card with their first choice crossed out and the course they actually took. Then a cut to them at work today.

  1. 0 to 3 sec: card held to camera, first choice crossed out
  2. 3 to 8 sec: "I didn't get it. I was crushed."
  3. 8 to 15 sec: cut to them in uniform, at work
  4. End: "Your second choice can still be the right one. Talk to us."
CaptionResults day is not the end of the story. Meet the students whose second choice became their career. Tag a friend who needs to see this.

Why it works: Every other page sells the "dream course". This one comforts the large group of students who miss it, right when they need it.

Enquiry flow
From a like

To a confirmed seat

Every step is planned, so a student who shows interest is never left waiting.

1
Sees a post or Reel

On the feed, Explore, a collab post or an ad.

2
Comments a keyword

"GUIDE", "FEES" or "ASK" sends an instant reply in DM.

3
Moves to WhatsApp

One tap to a counsellor. Chat labelled by course and stage.

4
Gets a follow up

Planned messages on day 1, day 3 and day 7 if there is no reply.

5
Books counselling

A call or office visit with the student and parent.

6
Confirms the seat

Documents, fees and college confirmation.

7
Becomes a story

Featured on the Admitted Wall, asked for a review and a referral.

8
Brings a friend

Referrals start the loop again at no ad cost.
