Cache

Digital scope and a six-month plan to launch Cache as a community people belong to, and to turn that belonging into regular visits, bookings and steady sales.

Prepared by Adoliy17 Oct 2026Plan window: Nov 2026 – Apr 2027
The idea

Cache is where Edappal keeps its best moments.

A cache is a place where valuable things are stored. That is the brand. Every visit, every conversation and every thing someone makes here becomes part of what Cache keeps. Online, we share that record, so the brand grows as the community does.

Community is the strategy and sales are the result. People who feel they belong visit more often, bring friends, and choose Cache for their gatherings and celebrations.

Signature plays

Eight ideas built for Cache

Each one starts offline and turns into content. That is the engine.

Pre-opening

The First Hundred

Cache has 100+ seats. Invite 100 founding members by application. Each gets their name on a brass tag on one seat, an invite to the soft launch, and first invites to everything on WhatsApp.

Creates 100 ambassadors before day one

Pre-opening

Before Anyone Else

In the final weeks of the build, small groups of 10–12 are invited for a preview walk and a first cup: shop owners, teachers, college reps, artists and community elders. They see it first and talk about it first.

Builds the local network and word of mouth before opening

Pre-opening

Under the Straw Roof

A six-part short video series on the build: bamboo workers, jute weaving, the straw roof, planting the lotus, the swans arriving, the pendant lights switching on.

People wait for an opening they watched being built

Pre-opening

Fill the Shelves

A book drive for the library. Every donated book gets a bookplate with the donor’s name. Goal: 500 books by opening. Each card inside has space for readers to leave a line.

Owners of the library come back to see their books

Monthly

The Long Table

Once a month, one long table, 30 seats, a set menu. Guests from different fields are seated together: founders, teachers, students, artists, returning NRIs. Paid booking, limited seats.

Turns Cache into Edappal’s networking table, and sells out

Every full moon

Moon Nights

The pond lit only by the moon and the pendant lights. Ghazals, poetry or silence. Seats by RSVP on WhatsApp. A fixed date people mark without being told.

A ritual tied to the place’s best feature

Ongoing

The Keeper’s Card

A hand-stamped card for regulars. After ten visits, your own mug is kept on the Cache shelf with your name. Card holders get first seats at Long Tables and members-only evenings.

Rewards return visits with belonging, not discounts

From month 3

Host a Night

Members propose an evening: chess, Mappila songs, a photo walk, a reading. Cache gives space and promotion. The walls rotate work made in Cache workshops, labelled “Made at Cache”.

Content direction

Place first, food in its place

Cache brings a London and US café culture to Edappal: a third place where people read, meet, make things and stay. That culture lives in the space and the people, not on the plate. Food-first content shrinks Cache to a menu. Place-first content shows the whole culture, so it leads. Food still matters for sales, so it always appears inside the space, with people around it.

Food-first

Looks like any café

  • Flat lays and latte art on plain tables
  • Easy to copy, hard to remember
  • Competes on menu and price
  • Shows the menu, misses the culture
Recommended · Place-first

Looks like only Cache

  • The space and people lead every frame
  • Food shown where it is eaten: majlis, pondside, under the lights
  • Competes on experience, holds a premium price
  • Fully London and US café culture: slow mornings, shared tables, books, art and live sets
